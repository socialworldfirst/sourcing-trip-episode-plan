WorldFirst · China Sourcing Trip, Tour 1 · voice, picture, graphics

Sourcing Trip Diaries, Tour 1: track sheet

The episode plan split into its layers. Voice is what the viewer hears (sync dialogue from a clip, or a voiceover still to record). Picture is what is on screen (A-roll, the person speaking, or B-roll, a scene with no speech needed). Graphics and other is everything built in post: title cards, the map, lower thirds, stat cards, subtitles, the end card and music. Timecodes are budgets, not cuts.

Owner Steven Chen · 15 Oct 2026 · Companion to index.html (the narrative plan) · Clip ids from the transcript sheets, picture unverified · Internal
01

How to read this

Three layers, one timeline. Where a cell says SYNC the sound comes from the clip named. Where it says VO the line is still to be recorded (scripts are in the narrative plan, section 09, and in note-to-joshua.md). A-ROLL means the speaker is on screen; B-ROLL means a scene with no speech, laid under voice from elsewhere. Timecodes total about 12 minutes of budget; the cut lands at 10 to 11 by trimming inside beats, not by dropping them.

No frame has been viewed. Every clip is a lead. B-roll marked 'hunt' is not in the transcript sheets because nobody spoke; it may or may not exist.
