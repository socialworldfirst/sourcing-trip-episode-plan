WorldFirst · China Sourcing Trip, Tour 1

The episode, step by step

Sourcing Trip Diaries, Tour 1, in plain words. Each step says what happens, what you hear, what you see and what text is on screen. Anything we do not have yet carries a red tag: not recorded (a voiceover still to do), footage unconfirmed (nobody has checked the cards), or to build (a graphic). Everything untagged was captured on the trip. The Footage line under each step is the shot-log check of 15 October: confirmed, partly, or missing. Each step now carries a storyboard strip: real frames from the crew's contact sheets, with the clip number, grade and best in and out point under each. Dashed red tiles are shots we do not have. Clip numbers in small grey type are for the editor.

Factory 3: Rongde

2 min 30 s

The heart of the film. A 16-floor factory that does everything under one roof, a lab that tests products for a year, and the best interview of the trip.

You hear
The welcome and the building. On the floor: nine lines, 2,000 kettles a day per line, a client asking for a kettle without the flower on it. In the lab: 'The first electric lunch box was tested for over a year, because of the lithium battery.' In Chinese: who pays for certification? 'If your volume is big, the factory does.' Then John, sitting down with Joshua: 30 per cent deposit, 'they come to see, they believe their eyes', 'like old friends', the Canton Fair is where they meet customers, and the twist: cheap lunch boxes on Amazon use plastic that is not fire resistant, so 'quality is what matters'. His advice: talk on WhatsApp first, then 'tell us which airport, we send a driver.'
